18 October 2026

Aigamuxa updated:

  • immune to weakening bodies no longer spam falling sound when weakened

Jojorwells updated:

  • Fullscreen has been modernised

SierraKomodo updated:

  • Megaphone usage now appears in round logs.

Sirofsirs11 updated:

  • Expeditionary Command has sent a checkers box to the Mess Hall to go with the board already there.
  • The checkers board now properly assigns itself as a machine to anyone who interacts with it. Or, in layman's terms, it automatically updates now.
  • You now open the chess board with shift-click instead of alt-click.
  • The SCG has reminded everyone that eating chess pieces is bad for your health. As such eating them is now impossible.
  • You can now draw multiple cards at once with the new Draw Multiple verb.

16 October 2026

Aigamuxa updated:

  • Armor now properly reduces knockdown, stutter and pain from unarmed attacks.
  • Pain from a kick to the groin or legs is now felt in the limb that was hit instead of a random one.
  • Prosthetic groins and species that cannot feel pain no longer react to groin kicks.

Banditoz updated:

  • The image from the ID card is now copied to the fake record when using the crew arrival announcement and records uplink item.
  • Your current mob's records are also no longer copied to the new fake record.
  • ID cards and records now have better looking images of their person!

05 October 2026

veeqies updated:

  • 10mm rubber: damage: 15 > 8; agony: 15 > 30
  • 7mm rubber: damage: 10 > 5; agony: 10 > 20
  • 12g beanbag: agony: 30 > 60

03 October 2026

veeqies updated:

  • Smartfridges can now be (un)secured when depowered

25 September 2026

Aigamuxa updated:

  • Snorting powders through masks is now impossible
  • Cooking kebabs, fortune cookies, brain cakes and nanopaste no longer runtimes
  • Diona nymphs no longer eat entire mugs - they now drink their contents
  • Closed beakers no longer can be poured into hydroponics

18 September 2026

Banditoz updated:

  • You can no longer fall asleep from inside of a cryo tube or stasis bag, unless you have a pre-existing condition.
  • You can no longer speak from inside of a cryo tube.
  • You can knock from inside of a cryo tube to make noise!
  • Up and down ladders now use a radial menu to prompt which direction you wish to go!
  • Add an advanced placement mode for cables, that makes it easier to lay cable in funky directions! Toggle it off and on with an action button at the top left while holding cable.

06 September 2026

Jorjorwells updated:

  • Off-ship antagonist spawns strip you of selected allergies.

  • "Sign Language" is now called Zurich Accord Sign Language.

25 August 2026

Banditoz updated:

  • Firearms now have randomized serial numbers! They can be filed off with a screwdriver.
  • Unskilled weapons expertise now has a chance to exclusively target mobs when fumbling a gun.
