Banditoz & Mucker updated:

  • Spider implants no longer spawn nursing spiders.
  • Spider implants only can spawn around 5 to 7 spiders before they stop working.
  • Spider implants no longer spawn spiders if the implantee is dead.
  • Spider implants now only allow up to 2 spiders to be alive at once near the implantee.
  • Tweaked the spider implant to only spawn a spider once every 180 seconds (up from 60 seconds), variance of 60 seconds.

Cakey updated:

  • The Torch now starts with two countermeasure missiles, to counter missiles with.
  • Missiles can now be spawned and fired at targets using the new missile build-mode.
  • High explosive missile warheads are now locked behind esoteric tech 4.
  • Added destructive tech origin values for missile parts.
  • Fixed targeting input not working on missile thrusters.

Mucker updated:

  • Added a shield diffuser to the missile launch tube.

Spacemanspark updated:

  • pAIs will no longer be stealth cloaked when picked up while resting.
  • pAIs will no longer be permanently slowed down in certain situations.
  • You now use non-help intents to fold a pAI back into card mode. Help intent will instead pet them.
  • pAIs can now understand and speak Yeosa'unathi, Gutter and Spacer.
  • pAI radio system broadcast range reduced to the tile they're standing on.

  • Adds a sprite for Eucharist wafers.

03 December 2025

Banditoz updated:

  • Adds the spider beacon implant! It's a 45 TC uplink item that spawns a random spider near the implantee every minute or so, until it's removed.

Cakey, Atebite updated:

  • Added overmap-based missile framework, along with a variety of payloads and thrusters. Missile frames can be constructed out of aluminium sheets, and can have various attachments provided by Supply, RnD, and antag uplinks.
  • Added missile interface machinery, used to remotely trigger conveyors, blast doors, and configure and arm missiles/probes.
  • Added a munitions control program to the NTNet, which collects all available missile interface machinery into a neat program, allowing you to remotely handle set-ups such as the probe launcher on the Torch.
  • Added a probe launcher to the Deck 1 Starboard Nacelle, replacing the auxiliary fuel store.

Qlonever updated:

  • Hanafuda cards are now available in character loadouts and game vendors.
  • Major arcana tarot cards use the correct articles now.

  • Shuttles now support space tiles, which change to the destination tile whenever the shuttle changes locations

KatTheFox updated:

  • Added three new pronoun sets: she/it, they/it, and he/it. They function exactly the same as the other combination pronoun sets like she/they.
